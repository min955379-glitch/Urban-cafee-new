About Urban Cafe & Restaurant

A Welcoming Family Environment in Mandian, Open Late Every Evening

Located at Jadoon Plaza Phase 2 in Mandian, Abbottabad, Urban Cafe & Restaurant offers a comfortable family environment designed for relaxed lunches, group dinners, and late-evening gatherings until 23:15.

Our kitchen prepares sizzling steaks, creamy pastas, Chinese favorites, gourmet burgers, oven-baked pizzas, freshly brewed coffee, and thick shakes for dine-in guests, takeaway pickup, and local delivery.

  • Comfortable Family Dining Environment
  • Open Late Until 23:15 Daily
  • Sizzling Steaks, Pastas & Chinese
  • Coffee, Mint Margarita & Thick Shakes
